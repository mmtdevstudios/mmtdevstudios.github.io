Privacy policy — BodyGame: Runner

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Developer and scope

This privacy policy applies to BodyGame: Runner for Android, developed by MMT Dev Studios.

Camera and motion detection

The camera is used during gameplay to detect body movements in real time. Images and derived body landmarks are processed locally on your device. They are not saved as files or transmitted to MMT Dev Studios or third parties.

Data collection and sharing

The game works offline and does not collect or transmit personal data to external servers. No camera images or motion data are shared with third parties.

Local storage and retention

Scores and preferences are stored on your device until you clear the application's storage or uninstall it. Camera images and motion detection data are processed temporarily during play and are not retained after the session.

Your choices

You can revoke camera access in Android settings. Motion-controlled gameplay requires camera access. You can delete locally stored scores and preferences by clearing app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
